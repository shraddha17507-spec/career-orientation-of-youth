Admission

How admission works, step by step

The steps below follow the Maharashtra Centralised Admission Process (CAP) used for most professional courses. Dates change every year, so always read the official notice.

    Documents checklist

    Tick what you already have. Your ticks stay on this device.

      Tips that save a seat

      • Make copies and scans of every document before the registration window opens.
      • Fill the option form with 10 or more colleges and courses in your real order of preference.
      • Note last dates for registration, verification, option form and fee payment.
      • Keep your phone number and email on the portal correct. All alerts go there.
      • Ask a teacher or senior to check your form before you submit.

      Money matters

      Scholarships and financial help

      In our survey 35.1% of students did not know about scholarships. Most are applied for online, once admission is confirmed.

      Eligibility, income limits and last dates change every year. Confirm them on the portal before you apply. Never pay anyone to apply for you.

      Resume checklist

      • One page, clean font, no photo needed for freshers.
      • Name, phone, professional email, city.
      • Education with year and percentage or CGPA.
      • Projects: what you built and what you learned.
      • Skills, certificates and achievements.
      • Save as PDF named YourName_Resume.pdf.

      Common interview questions

      • Tell me about yourself.
